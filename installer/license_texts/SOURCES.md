Components of the program's own Windows CalculiX `ccx_spooles.exe` (built by `installer/windows/build_ccx.ps1` from the pinned sources; every source archive and every toolchain package is pinned by SHA-256 in `installer/windows/build_ccx.sh` and `ccx_toolchain.lock`; checked on the binary with `objdump -p` and `strings` 18. 11. 2026): CalculiX 2.23 (GPL-2.0, "version 2" in the headers of the sources; changed by two patches, and SPOOLES by a third one, that travel with every package in `installer/windows/patches/` (the Linux build uses the same patches in `installer/linux/patches/`) and are the source offer for the modified program together with the pinned CalculiX archive in `sources/` and the build script: `ccx_2.23_ucrt_build.patch` = a build fix without solver code, `ccx_2.23_spooles_serial_solve.patch` = the change of the solver logic of 20. 11. 2026 (revised 21. 11. 2026) – the multi-threaded triangular solve of SPOOLES is replaced by the serial one and every multi-threaded factorization is checked by a test solve (row-wise backward error, limit 1e-12; the repeat on one thread is checked too) and repeated when the check fails, `ccx -v` prints `FEA_SPOOLES_SERIAL_SOLVE=1`, `FEA_SPOOLES_FACTOR_CHECK=2` and `FEA_SPOOLES_ORDERED_ASSEMBLY=1`; the manifest.json in the zip carries the digests of the patches), SPOOLES 2.2 (public domain, changed by the patch `spooles_2.2_ordered_assembly.patch` of 21. 11. 2026: the sums of the multi-threaded factorization are made in an order that does not depend on the timing, the lists that hand the update chevrons from one thread to another are always locked and counted; its Harwell-Boeing reader iohb.c carries the NIST permission notice), ARPACK-ng 3.9.1 (BSD-3), reference BLAS / LAPACK 3.12.0 (modified BSD), the GCC 16.2.0 runtime libgcc, libgfortran (with libbacktrace) and libgomp (GPL-3.0 with the Runtime Library Exception), libquadmath (LGPL-2.1 or later: 19 objects ARE inside the Windows binary, pulled in by libgfortran;
